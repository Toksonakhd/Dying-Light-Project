Dockets are codes you can redeem for in-game items available in the Dying Light series.

Docket codes are great at disguising… Sometimes they appear in the form of 4x3 hexadecimal codes like abcd-1234-ef56. You can find them hidden across our channels — in social media posts, videos, and graphics we publish regularly.
